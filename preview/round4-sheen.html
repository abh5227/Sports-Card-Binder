Round 4 — the sheen, before the frame

The frame variant is held constant at D. The cards, pockets, gutters, margins and every Round 1–3 decision are constant. Only the sheen model varies.

This bar is 150 px — one card. If it does not match, your zoom is not 100%.

Why this comes before the frame. The two models differ at the spine. A highlight sweeping unbroken across the gutter reinforces “one grid with a wider gutter down the middle”. A highlight that breaks at the spine asserts two sheets in one object. That is the same question the frame variants are being judged on, so the sheen has to be held still first.

Baseline — what Round 4 shipped, and the bug

Each sheet given its own identical copy of the same gradient. Measured at 62% across on both pages, L 0.01932 on each — two identical highlights side by side. This is the Round 3 nine-identical-speculars problem one level up.

Model 1 — flat spread, one continuous sweep

Both sheets coplanar. One gradient across the full spread width, so the left sheet sits on the rising limb and the far right on the falling limb. The direct one-level-up application of the Round 3 fix.

Model 1b — flat sweep, peak moved onto the right page

The untuned stops put Model 1's peak on the spine itself. This is the same continuous sweep with the peak where it was intended, so the comparison is not tuned against it.

Model 2 — shallow V, a band per sheet

The sheets are not coplanar; a real spread tilts each page toward the spine. Each catches its own band, differing in position and intensity, the left brighter because it faces the light.
