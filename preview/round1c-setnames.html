32 characters fit on one line in 134px of usable scrim width. 96.4% of all 1,210 sets fit on one line; 44 do not. The very worst measures 195px — 1.46 lines, so two, never three. No pre-1990 set needs a second line; 4.2% of 2015-onward sets do.

3 — Year and set on one line

A change the two-line case suggests: 1988 alone occupies a whole line for four characters. Merging year and set recovers a line on every card, and on the worst case absorbs the wrap almost entirely.

4 — The scrim's top hairline

Over a dark card the old gradient scrim had no boundary — dark panel on dark card, text floating. The fix is a flat panel with a 1px top hairline at rgba(232,228,220,.18). The gradient had to go with it: a hairline drawn on a fading edge floats above nothing. This is a change to the scrim only — the card's own lit edge stays deferred.
