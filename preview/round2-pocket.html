Round 2 — the pocket

Five treatments, from no sleeve at all to clearly a physical sleeve. Same cards, same geometry, same light from the upper left. Only the plastic varies. The card treatment is Round 1 as committed and is untouched.

This bar is 150 px — one card wide. If it does not match, your zoom is not 100%.

0 — The three that matter

The baseline, the treatment the theory predicted, and the treatment the measurements produced. Everything below is the working. Judge this strip.

What was measured first

Edge luminance of all nine card images, sampled per side at pocket scale (150 × 210), against the page at #171A1F, L = 0.0080. This is what the sleeve has to fix.

Two corrections fall out of this. Crosby is not a near-black card — its border is silver, L 0.54–0.64, an 11.7:1 step against the page. What is dark is its bottom third, which is why it was the right card for the Round 1 scrim test and is the wrong one here. And Nash and Judge carry black scan background on two edges each — a crop defect, not a card property. Any sleeve judged against those is judged against a bar of black. Both are excluded below.

1 — The near-black card, all five treatments

Daniel Jones, 2023 Panini Prizm Blue Wave. Edges L 0.12–0.20, bottom edge L 0.006 — a 1.04:1 step against the page, which is to say none. This one cell is the whole round.

2 — The light-bordered card, all five treatments

Kevin Durant, white border, L 0.58–0.63. Watch for a doubled edge: card white / lit well / rim highlight stacking into mush.

3 — The empty pocket, all five treatments

You do not fill every page. An empty pocket is the treatment with nothing to hide behind: if the sleeve only convinces with a card in it, it is a frame, not a sleeve.

4 — The scrim up, on the near-black card

The reveal and the sleeve are two light-edged horizontals a few pixels apart. The scrim is inset to the card, so between its hairline and the pocket's bottom rim there is 7px of lit plastic.

5 — Per treatment, in context

Each treatment carrying dark, dark-uniform, light, light-silver and empty side by side — the comparison that catches a treatment which fixes one card by ruining another.

Four pockets, one of them empty. Not a page — page layout is Round 3 and nothing here should pre-decide it.
